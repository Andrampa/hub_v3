# DIEM product launch strategy context

## Purpose

This folder is the working source of truth for planning the coordinated launch
and dissemination of three public DIEM products before the end of the year. It
keeps communication decisions, audiences, sequencing, ownership and campaign
assets together without mixing them into product implementation documentation.

## Products in scope

- The new DIEM Hub, the public front door for discovering DIEM evidence.
- The new Household Survey Explorer, the specialist experience for exploring
  household monitoring results.
- EVE 2.0, including better flood data, relevant data from other FAO teams,
  VISTA, Floodex and field data collection, validation and impact-assessment
  capabilities. VISTA is part of the EVE 2.0 launch, not a separate product.

## Strategic decisions

- Use one shared narrative about a renewed DIEM evidence ecosystem, while
  retaining distinct product messages and calls to action.
- Position the Hub as the front door. The Explorer and EVE remain specialist
  analytical products reached directly or through the Hub.
- Run two main communication streams: Hub plus Household Survey Explorer; and
  EVE 2.0, which includes VISTA.
- Publish the Hub and Household Survey Explorer quietly after the necessary
  clearances, replace their legacy versions, and then begin coordinated
  communication after a short observation period.
- Complete launch, dissemination and the first adoption activities before the
  end of the year. Use relative milestones rather than fixed dates until the
  internal calendar is agreed.
- Follow the launch with audience- and country-specific training, intensive
  champion-country support and an appropriate lighter support offer for all
  DIEM countries.

## Relative milestone model

| Milestone | Purpose |
|---|---|
| T0 Align | Confirm narrative, names, audiences, roles, channels and asset list. |
| T1 Validate | Present the Hub to Team 1A, incorporate relevant feedback and prepare the OCC package. |
| T2 Clear and prepare | Obtain OCC clearance for the Hub and finalize communication assets, distribution lists and event materials. |
| T3 Quiet publication | Publish the Hub and Household Survey Explorer, replace legacy versions and check the live journeys. |
| T4 Internal activation | Hold the OER event and use internal screens, intranet and FAO mailing channels. |
| T5 External campaign | Activate the two campaign streams through DIEM, OCC, ESS, social, newsletter and partner channels. |
| T6 Adoption | Deliver targeted onboarding, support champion countries intensively, offer lighter support to all DIEM countries and review early use. |

T0 through T6 should be completed, or firmly initiated where training continues,
before the end of the year. Milestones may overlap once owners and dependencies
are clear.

## Audience and channel logic

| Audience | Priority offer | Primary action | Main route |
|---|---|---|---|
| All FAO Representatives | DIEM Hub | Share the Hub with relevant national counterparts. | Country-specific email pack |
| Active household-monitoring countries | Hub and Household Survey Explorer | Explore and share the relevant country and round; nominate trainees. | FAO Representatives, regional advisers, DIEM network |
| Flood-hazard countries | EVE 2.0 and VISTA | Explore the relevant country and identify operational users. | FAO Representatives, emergency and GIS networks |
| FAO staff and OER colleagues | Complete ecosystem | Understand the offer and route users to the right product. | OER event, intranet, screens, FAO latest mailing list |
| Governments, partners, researchers and donors | Public evidence and analytical tools | Use, cite and share relevant evidence. | News, web, newsletters and social media |

## Training direction

- Hub usage should be supported by concise onboarding material; a formal
  training course is unlikely to be necessary.
- EVE training should cover the core application, Risk module and field data
  collection and validation workflows.
- Household Survey Explorer training should cover country and round selection,
  thematic exploration, interpretation, analysis tools, exports and sharing.
- Associate countries with EVE, Explorer or both according to monitoring
  activity, flood exposure, operational need, analyst capacity and expressed
  interest.
- Where budget and demand justify it, Andrea or regional advisers may deliver
  intensive two- to three-day missions in one or two champion countries.
- Without mission funding, Andrea may deliver a two-day online training of
  trainers: one day for EVE 2.0 and one day for the Household Survey Explorer.
- Use the OER launch and country outreach to collect training interest and
  identify named focal points.
- Give all DIEM countries access to lighter support such as recordings, short
  online orientations, guidance, office hours or regional sessions.
